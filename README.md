## 🚀 Featured product: Vissotia

<div align="center">

![Vissotia](https://img.shields.io/badge/VISSOTIA-PropTech_%2B_AI-0F766E?style=for-the-badge)
![Finalist](https://img.shields.io/badge/FINALIST-Premios_Fotocasa_Pro_2026-D4A017?style=for-the-badge)

</div>

**Vissotia** is an intelligent real estate platform I am developing to transform fragmented property data into a single, actionable commercial workspace. It brings together Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and real estate business knowledge to help agencies, property developers, investors and commercial teams make better-informed decisions.

### The problem it addresses

Real estate teams often work with disconnected data sources, manual document processing and commercial decisions that are difficult to reproduce or explain. Vissotia creates a shared source of truth for each property project, connecting data, analysis, content and decision support in one workflow.

### Solutions provided by Vissotia

| Property intelligence | Commercial productivity | Responsible AI |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Machine Learning valuation, area analysis and enrichment with **Catastro, INE and Google Maps** | Document OCR, project memory, contextual chat and AI-assisted commercial content generation | Traceability, guardrails, observability, audit records and human oversight for AI-supported decisions |

The platform is designed to move from raw property information to **clear commercial reasoning and concrete actions**, while preserving the context and evidence behind every recommendation.

> 🏆 **Vissotia was selected as a finalist in the Premios Fotocasa Pro 2026**, recognising its innovative approach to applying data and AI to the real estate sector.
